How Utah's ADU rules work in 2026

Internal ADUs (inside your home's footprint, like a basement apartment) have been a permitted use in Utah's residential zones since 2021. Detached ADUs got a major boost on October 1, 2026, when SB284 began requiring most larger Utah cities to allow them as a permitted use on single-family lots of 11,000 sq ft or more, with capped parking and no conditional use permit. Cities can still regulate size, height, setbacks, owner occupancy, and short-term rentals, and HOAs can still prohibit detached units.
