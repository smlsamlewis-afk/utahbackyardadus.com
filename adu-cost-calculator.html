What's included in an ADU price in Utah?

Our ranges are all-in project costs: design and engineering, permits and plan review, site work and foundation, the structure, utility connections, and interior finishes. The biggest swing factors are size, finish level, and how far the ADU sits from your existing water, sewer, and power. Read the full breakdown in our Utah ADU cost guide, or see how homeowners finance an ADU.

Does my lot qualify?

As of October 1, 2026, most larger Utah cities must allow a detached ADU on single-family lots of 11,000 sq ft or more. Take the 60-second lot check or read what Utah's new ADU law changed.
