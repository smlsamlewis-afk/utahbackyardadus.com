Lehi ADU rules at a glance

  • Detached backyard ADUs are allowed on Lehi lots of 11,000 sq ft or more under Utah's SB284 (effective Oct. 1, 2026).
  • Internal ADUs (basement apartments) are a permitted use in Utah residential zones on lots over 6,000 sq ft.
  • Typical all-in cost in Utah: garage conversion $50K–$120K, modular $120K–$200K, custom stickbuilt $120K–$300K.

Lehi already allowed detached ADUs, but only on lots of 14,520 sq ft (a third of an acre) or more. In July 2026 the city lowered that minimum to the state's 11,000 sq ft threshold, which city staff estimated could grow the number of eligible lots by up to 20%.

Lehi ADU FAQ

What size lot do I need for a detached ADU in Lehi?

11,000 sq ft. Lehi lowered its minimum from 14,520 sq ft in July 2026 to match Utah's new state threshold.

Do I have to live on the property in Lehi?

Yes. Lehi requires the owner to live in either the main home or the detached ADU.

How far does a detached ADU have to be from the house in Lehi?

Lehi's updated rules require detached units to sit at least 6 feet from the existing home.
